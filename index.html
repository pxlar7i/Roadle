How to play

Every day there's one new junction. Get the rush-hour traffic through it quickly, safely and cheaply.

  1. Read the junction. Tap Forecast to see where traffic wants to go. Lettered badges mark each road.
  2. Build. Pick a tool, tap a glowing spot on the map. Everything you place costs money.
  3. Test. Press Test to send traffic through. Tweak and repeat as often as you like.
  4. Submit. One official rush hour decides today's score.

Score

You start from a flow score (up to 1000) based on how long people wait — the worst-served road counts extra, and buses and trams carry lots of people. Then you lose points for every crash and for the cost of what you built. Beat the city engineer's score for three stars.
